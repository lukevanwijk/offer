Important notice

Submitting this form doesn't create a contract, and it doesn't commit you or the seller to anything. A sale is only binding once both parties have signed a contract of sale.

Before you sign, you should get the Seller Disclosure Statement for the property. For residential contracts in Queensland (other than auction sales), buyers usually have a cooling-off period of 5 business days. If you end the contract during that period, a termination penalty of 0.25% of the purchase price applies.

Elders Real Estate Shailer Park acts for the seller. We'll present your offer to the seller, but we can't guarantee it will be accepted. We recommend you get your own legal and financial advice before signing anything.

Privacy

We collect the details you enter here, including any ID you upload, so we can present your offer and prepare a contract if it's accepted. We may share them with the seller, solicitors or conveyancers, and the service providers that help us run this form, some of which store data overseas, including in the United States.
